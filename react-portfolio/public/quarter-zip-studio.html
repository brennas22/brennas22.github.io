The neck foundation grades in complete rib repeats; lift and nape dip grade in short-row pairs. D is the initial cast-on span, not the complete neckline perimeter. Armhole depth counts the front raglan-edge rows after the picked-up front short rows. Open How calculated for the row breakdown. Dashed points need a garment landmark or unfinished construction.

Measurement definitions and sources

Body measurements and ease follow the Craft Yarn Council and Knitting & Crochet Guild. Schematic points and count-based finished dimensions follow The Knitting Guild Association. Armhole depth is vertical, as explained by Patty Lyons. Extra back-neck length from short rows is distinct from the opening itself; see Tin Can Knits.
